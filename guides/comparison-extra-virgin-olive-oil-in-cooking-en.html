Comparison: extra virgin olive oil in cooking

Comparison: extra virgin olive oil in cooking

Published : Mai 2026 · 5 min read

A central element of Mediterranean gastronomy, extra virgin olive oil sublimates the flavors of your dishes while providing an incomparable texture.

Excellence in cooking

Whether used for gentle frying, roasting, or simply dressing, it withstands heat well and magnifies fresh ingredients.

Culinary tips

  • Cooking: Perfect up to 180°C (smoke point) without losing its qualities.
  • Seasoning: A drizzle at the end of cooking reveals the aromas of vegetables or fish.
  • Baking: An excellent substitute for butter for lighter and moister cakes.
"The secret of a great chef often lies in the quality of their oil."

Perfect pairings

Vary the pleasures by associating ripe fruity oils for desserts and intense green fruitiness for grilled foods.
