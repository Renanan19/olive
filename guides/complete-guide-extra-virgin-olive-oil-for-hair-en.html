Olive Oil for Hair

Olive oil is a traditional hair treatment: it coats the strand, softens and tames frizz. Here's how to use it as an oil treatment, and what it can — or can't — do for your hair.

Long used as a hair treatment, olive oil coats the strand and limits breakage. It's not a miracle product, but a good emollient for dry hair and split ends — as long as you use it in moderation.

Key facts
  • Olive oil coats the hair shaft: it softens, reduces frizz and limits breakage.
  • Typical use: pre-shampoo oil treatment on lengths and ends, 30 min to overnight, then shampoo.
  • It is heavy: a small amount is enough, and a double shampoo may be needed.
  • No strong evidence it makes hair 'grow'; its benefit is cosmetic (coating, softening).

What olive oil does for hair

Olive oil is an emollient that coats the hair shaft. It smooths the cuticle, reduces frizz, adds flexibility and limits mechanical breakage during detangling. This is especially useful for dry, thick, curly or damaged hair.

The pre-shampoo oil treatment

The most effective method: warm a little olive oil, apply to the lengths and especially the ends (little on the roots if your scalp is oily), leave 30 minutes to overnight under a towel, then wash with shampoo. A pre-shampoo oil reduces porosity and protects the strand during washing.

Scalp and ends

A gentle olive oil scalp massage can help soften flakes and ease the feeling of dryness. On split ends, oil doesn't 'repair' the cut strand but temporarily coats it for a smoother look.

Amount and rinsing

Olive oil is heavy: a small amount is enough, otherwise hair looks greasy and dull. Work shampoo in before wetting to remove the oil more easily, and plan a second shampoo if needed.

What not to expect

Contrary to some claims, there's no strong evidence that olive oil makes hair grow faster. Its benefit is cosmetic: coating, softness, less frizz. For hair loss or a scalp problem, see a professional.

Frequently asked questions

Is olive oil good for hair?

Yes for cosmetic use: it coats the strand, softens, reduces frizz and limits breakage, especially on dry or damaged hair. Used as a pre-shampoo oil treatment, it's a simple, effective care.

How do you do an olive oil hair treatment?

Warm a little oil, apply to lengths and ends, leave 30 min to overnight under a towel, then wash with shampoo (twice if needed).

Does olive oil make hair grow?

There is no strong evidence it speeds growth. Its benefit is cosmetic: it coats and softens the strand. For hair loss, see a professional.

Organic or regular olive oil for hair?

For hair use, the difference is negligible: quality and freshness matter most. A good extra virgin works perfectly.

How do you get olive oil out of hair?

Apply shampoo to dry or barely damp hair to emulsify the oil, massage, then rinse. Repeat once more if hair stays greasy.
