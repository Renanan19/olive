Discovering extra virgin olive oil for hair

Discovering extra virgin olive oil for hair

Published : Mai 2026 · 5 min read

For radiantly healthy hair, using extra virgin olive oil offers a powerful alternative to chemical hair care products.

A natural hair mask

Its moisturizing agents penetrate the hair fiber to repair split ends and restore suppleness and shine to dull hair.

Actions on the hair

  • Nutrition: Restores the lipid film of dry and damaged hair.
  • Scalp: Soothes itching and helps reduce dandruff.
  • Growth: Massaging the scalp with the oil stimulates hair growth.
"The strength of an olive tree transmitted to your hair."

How to apply it?

As an oil bath before shampooing, leave it on for at least 30 minutes, or overnight under a warm towel for maximum effectiveness.
