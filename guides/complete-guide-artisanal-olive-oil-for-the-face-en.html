Complete Guide: artisanal olive oil for the face

Complete Guide: artisanal olive oil for the face

Published : Mai 2026 · 5 min read

A true ancestral beauty elixir, artisanal olive oil is a formidable natural cosmetic treatment for facial skin.

Deep hydration

Packed with squalene and vitamins A and E, it deeply nourishes the epidermis, prevents skin aging, and soothes irritations.

Dermatological benefits

  • Anti-aging: Fights against free radicals responsible for wrinkles.
  • Makeup remover: Gently dissolves stubborn makeup (even waterproof).
  • Repairing: Aids in healing and calms dry or atopic skin.
"Nature offers the best cosmetics to those who know how to use them."

Beauty ritual

Apply a few drops at night on clean skin, massaging gently to stimulate microcirculation and brighten the complexion.
