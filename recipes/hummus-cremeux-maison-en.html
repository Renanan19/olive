Homemade Creamy Hummus

Homemade Creamy Hummus: a detailed olive-oil recipe with ingredients, step-by-step method, tips and variations.

Prep : 15 min · Cook : 0 min · Rest : 20 min · Difficulty : Easy

Homemade Creamy Hummus puts olive oil at the center of the plate: it brings aroma, texture and cohesion while keeping the method approachable.

Ingredients

  • L'Or Vert extra virgin olive oil
  • mellow garlic
  • lemon juice
  • sea salt
  • fresh herbs
  • chickpeas
  • an acidic touch: mild vinegar or lemon juice
  • a crunchy element: seeds, nuts or toasted bread

Step-by-step method

  1. Prepare aromatic ingredients very finely to obtain a smooth, expressive sauce.
  2. Pour olive oil in a thin stream while mixing to create a stable, glossy emulsion.
  3. Add acidity gradually, then taste before salting because each oil reacts differently.
  4. Rest chilled so garlic, herbs or spices infuse without becoming aggressive.
  5. Loosen with a spoonful of cold water if the texture becomes too thick.
  6. Serve with vegetables, toasted bread, fish or white meat depending on intensity.

The role of olive oil

Olive oil is not just a fat: it carries aromas, softens texture and gives a clean finish. For this recipe, choose a fruity but balanced oil that supports the ingredients without masking them.

Success tips

  • Taste before serving: oil softens while acidity brightens.
  • Add the final drizzle off the heat.
  • Serve on a warm plate to keep aromas open.

Variations and pairings

For variation, simply change the oil profile: mild and round for a family-style result, green and peppery for a more gastronomic plate. Fresh herbs or citrus zest are often enough.

Storage

Refrigerate if the recipe contains delicate ingredients, then bring back toward room temperature 10 minutes before serving. Crisp preparations are best the same day.
